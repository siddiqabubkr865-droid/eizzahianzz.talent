Our Story & Heritage

Excellence begins with character.

Eizzah represents the eleventh batch of Noorul Huda Central for Excellence, Madanoor — a place where Islamic scholarship and contemporary education are woven into one purposeful journey. Located in Puttur Taluk, Dakshina Kannada District, Karnataka, our institution is renowned for shaping disciplined scholars and modern leaders.

Across the classroom, the field and the stage, our batch grows through curiosity, service, brotherhood, and the steady pursuit of Ihsan (excellence).
